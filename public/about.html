Privacy, credits & licenses

An independent collection by Johnson Lee, showing three attributed community reconstructions of LEGO® vehicle sets.

This is a model viewer, not a build guide. Geometry, printed details and counts may differ from the physical set. Use the original LEGO® booklets for physical assembly. This viewer does not certify connections, mechanisms or safety.

Privacy and requests

The application has no account system, analytics, tracking cookies or application backend. It does not read or save visitor progress. Any progress saved by an earlier version remains in that browser’s storage until the visitor clears it; this viewer leaves it untouched.

Models, inventories, images and scripts are loaded from this site on GitHub Pages. Fonts come from your device; no Google Fonts request is made. Opening an external link visits that provider’s site, where its own practices apply. GitHub Pages handles requests for the hosted site; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

The LDraw “Official Model Repository” name refers to community models of official sets. It does not imply LEGO endorsement. Original instruction artwork belongs to the LEGO Group and is linked on LEGO.com. The public viewer does not distribute the booklets’ full instruction sequences, per-step callout datasets or page artwork.

Catalog data

Part, color and element crosswalk data comes from Rebrickable Downloads. Rebrickable permits reuse of these downloads with source acknowledgment. The retained Sián, Chiron and Porsche crosswalks identify source CSVs, retrieval dates and hashes. Their data is excluded from the application’s MIT license. Model inventories shown in the viewer are counted from the licensed CAD instances, not copied instruction callouts.
